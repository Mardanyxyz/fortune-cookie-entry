Entrants write one fortune cookie message aimed at a software engineer, a single line of at most 120 characters. A judge model reads the entries two at a time and picks the funnier one in each pair, and the message that wins its pairs most often takes first place. Lines are weighed on being a real fortune cookie message for an engineer, on how hard the joke lands on a first read, and on freshness, so a stock joke from a mug or a conference slide loses to a line nobody has seen before.
